# Continuity and QA Checklist

## Story
- [ ] Every major event has a cause and consequence.
- [ ] No unexplained character entrance, time jump or prop appearance.
- [ ] Character goals and stakes remain understandable.
- [ ] Dialogue matches the approved screenplay verbatim.

## Character and assets
- [ ] Same face, hair, outfit, accessories, scale and materials across shots.
- [ ] All visible characters, environments, props and effects are 3D.
- [ ] No missing meshes, textures, rigs or broken links.
- [ ] Rounded mitten hands match the character bible.

## Animation and camera
- [ ] Foot planting and weight shifts read correctly.
- [ ] Hands maintain prop contact.
- [ ] No sliding, floating, clipping or intersections.
- [ ] Facial acting and lip sync support dialogue.
- [ ] Camera movement is smooth, motivated and maintains screen direction.

## Sound and edit
- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible and not masked.
- [ ] Foley, ambience, effects and music are timed.
- [ ] Scene and shot transitions are clean.
- [ ] Final runtime is 300–600 seconds.

## Render
- [ ] FPS and frame count match project settings.
- [ ] No black frames, missing textures or broken shots.
- [ ] Audio is present, synced and unclipped.
- [ ] Final output has been watched or reviewed, not merely exported.
