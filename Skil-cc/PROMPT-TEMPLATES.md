# SKIL-CC V4 — Full 3D Prompt Templates

These are production briefs for a 3D modeler, Blender workflow or compatible 3D asset generator. A text prompt does not guarantee a clean, rigged, animation-ready model.

## Character model brief
Create an original stylized full-3D human cartoon character named [NAME]. Full volumetric model visible from every angle, [HEIGHT/PROPORTION], [FACE], [SKIN MATERIAL], [EYES/BROWS/MOUTH], [MODELED HAIR], wearing [OUTFIT/MATERIALS/COLOURS], [SHOES] and [ACCESSORIES]. Keep anatomy consistent and silhouette readable. Hands are smooth rounded 3D mitten forms without separated fingers. Neutral A-pose or T-pose, grounded feet, front/side/back/three-quarter views, studio lighting, no text or logos. Deliver clean topology, UVs, named materials, sensible origin and rig-ready structure.

## Character rig brief
Rig [CHARACTER ID] with root, pelvis, spine, chest, neck, head, clavicles, upper arms, forearms, wrists, thighs, shins and feet. Add facial controls or shape keys for [EXPRESSIONS]. Maintain volume during bending. Include foot planting, IK/FK where supported, clean deformation weights and named controls. Test walk, reach, sit, turn and prop-contact poses. Keep mitten hands simple and fingerless.

## Environment brief
Build a complete 3D [LOCATION] set with real geometry: [ARCHITECTURE], [TERRAIN], [VEGETATION], [FURNITURE] and [STORY PROPS]. Define dimensions using a human scale reference. Provide entrances/exits, depth, consistent material palette and motivated lighting. No flat background cards.

## Prop brief
Create a reusable 3D [PROP] with dimensions [SIZE], materials [MATERIALS], pivot/origin [LOCATION], interaction points [GRAB/CONTACT AREAS] and clean topology. It must be usable in animation and maintain scale across shots.

## Cinematic shot brief
Scene [SCENE ID], shot [SHOT ID], duration [SECONDS]. Dramatic purpose: [PURPOSE]. Character blocking: [POSITIONS/ACTIONS]. Prop interaction: [CAUSE AND EFFECT]. Camera: [SHOT SIZE], [LENS], [HEIGHT/ANGLE], [MOVE/PATH], focus [SUBJECT]. Lighting: [SOURCES/COLOUR]. VFX and audio: [CUES]. Preserve model, outfit, set and screen-direction continuity.

## Animation brief
Animate [CHARACTER ID] performing [ACTION] from [START POSE] to [END POSE] over [DURATION]. Establish intention and eye target, then anticipation, weight shift, action, contact, follow-through and recovery. Specify root movement, body mechanics, arm/leg arcs, facial acting, hand contact, foot planting and timing. Avoid foot sliding, prop penetration, sudden snaps and weightless motion.

## VFX brief
Create a physically motivated 3D [EFFECT] caused by [EVENT]. Define source, direction, scale, lifetime, interaction with light/surfaces, density and fade-out. Keep faces and story-critical action visible.

## 10-minute production prompt
Develop an original 10:00 full-3D animated film about [PREMISE]. Build a causal story with protagonist goal, opposition, escalating complications, meaningful cost, climax and consequence. Deliver logline, synopsis, character bible, location/prop list, timed screenplay, scene breakdown, shot list with timecodes, camera plan, animation plan, lighting/VFX plan, audio cue sheet and continuity checklist. Target [24 OR 30] fps and calculate exact frame count. Every character, environment, prop and effect must be volumetric 3D. No 2D character cards or billboard characters. Do not copy a creator's exact shots, characters or script.

## Negative constraints
No 2D sprites, flat character cards, billboard characters, missing back-side geometry, flat environment backdrops, uncanny photoreal faces, plastic skin, random extra limbs, mesh intersections, floating feet, sliding hands, texture flicker, costume drift, camera clipping, logos or watermarks.
